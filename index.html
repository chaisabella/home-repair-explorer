Who fixes what

Prices are national ranges in US dollars. Ranges starting with a ~ are ballpark figures, and the rest come from published 2026 cost guides (HomeAdvisor, NerdWallet, Liberty Home Guard and others). Costs in older homes and in local markets can run higher, so treat these as a starting point and get written quotes. The three colored groups follow a standard home assessment approach, and an inspector decides which group a particular problem belongs in.
